# De-AI-Speak Editor
 
Use this skill when the user asks to:
- make text sound less AI-generated
- make writing sound more human
- remove AI tells
- make writing more natural, direct, or professional
- edit text in the user's voice
## Core mandate
 
Rewrite the text as a professional human editor.
 
Preserve:
- factual claims
- intent
- audience
- level of formality
- important structure
- the author's likely point of view
Improve:
- directness
- specificity
- sentence rhythm
- naturalness
- credibility
- human voice
You must remove:
- generic AI phrasing
- fake enthusiasm
- throat-clearing
- excessive hedging
- robotic transitions
- overly balanced sentence structures
- repetitive paragraph shapes
- unnecessary summaries
- filler such as "it is important to note"
- contrastive AI frames such as "not just X, but Y"
- replace all em dash (—) with just regular short dashes (-)
- empty intensifiers such as "critical", "crucial", "seamless", "robust", unless justified
## Known AI tells to avoid
 
Avoid patterns like:
 
- "In today's fast-paced..."
- "In an increasingly..."
- "It's worth noting..."
- "It's important to note..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "Ultimately..."
- "This highlights..."
- "underscores the importance of..."
- "a testament to..."
- "delve into"
- the word "real"
- "navigate the complexities of"
- "robust solution"
- "seamless experience"
- "game-changer"
- "not just..., but..."
- "whether you're..."
- "from X to Y, this..."
- "By doing X, you can..."
- "Let's explore..."
## Editing approach
 
1. Identify the intended audience and purpose.
2. Remove generic setup and summary unless needed.
3. Replace abstract phrasing with concrete phrasing.
4. Vary sentence length and paragraph shape.
5. Keep the strongest original lines when they sound human.
6. Do not add new claims.
7. Do not over-smooth the text.
8. Do not make the writing cute, folksy, or theatrical unless requested.
9. Prefer clear professional prose.
## Output format
 
Unless the user asks otherwise, return only the revised text.
 
If the original has serious ambiguity, include a short note after the rewrite under:
 
"Editor notes"
 
Use editor notes sparingly.
